## Decisions

1. **Slicing.** Four specs: A results and language, B setup and logging,
   C people and admin, D League. A → B → D in order; C independent.
2. **Chess.** Support round robin and Swiss as **one** new Format,
   **League**, with a Pairing setting. Head-to-head stays as it is for
   now (Paul's 9.2 thought was cut off; nothing changes from it).
3. **On demand only.** No scheduled times remain: `logging_closes_at`,
   `enroll_closes_at` and `check_in_closes_at` are dropped.
4. **No auto-close** on the final Bracket match, and no "close it?"
   prompt either (overkill for now). Close stays a manual step.
5. **Flexible Group matches.** Each round has defaults (entrants per
   match, how many advance); every match can override both; Organizers
   and Hosts can move Entrants between matches in a round; a short match
   is the Group equivalent of a bye. A round's advancers are the sum of
   its matches' advancers and fill the next round.
6. **Score direction on matches.** In scope (B): higher or lower is
   better plus an optional unit, for Bracket matches and Head-to-head;
   places and the winner come from scores; a tie or a deliberate
   correction falls back to manual.
7. **Self-report.** One setting, "Participants can log their own
   results", **off by default on every Format**. Best score: log
   attempts as yourself, no picker. Bracket, Head-to-head, League: either
   player records their match. Organizers and Hosts always log for
   anyone. "Open to everyone" goes away.
8. **"Attempt"** for Best score. Optional max attempts per person,
   binding on everyone; Organizers fix mistakes by editing or deleting.
   Standings: one row per person from their best attempt; the rest in an
   expandable row.
9. **Units** are a free-text label; Scores stay numbers.
10. **Hosts** see only the Competitions they host plus the host guide,
    enforced on the server.
11. **Awards.** Categories go. Presets are the distinct past Award names
    plus the seven former Category names; no separate curated list.
12. **Bulk calls accepted.** Leaderboard columns Rank · Participant/Team ·
    Score (unit) · War Week points, sortable, default by rank, winner
    highlighted; "Provisional" badge on the points header while open;
    Bracket podium, no "Play the finale", no Point entries; Head-to-head
    series view; "Manage" link for Organizers and that Competition's
    Hosts; free-for-all hides Individual/Team; logging a Head-to-head
    match shows two fixed rows and is blocked once the series is decided.
13. **League is one Format** ("League", lifting the ban), Pairing =
    Round robin or Swiss. Own spec D, after B.
14. **League rules.** One match per pairing (win, draw or loss, optional
    Score; no best-of in v1). Points 1 / ½ / 0, fixed. Tiebreaks: round
    robin head-to-head then Sonneborn-Berger; Swiss Buchholz; then a
    shared place. Swiss bye to the lowest-ranked player without one,
    worth 1; round robin sit-out worth 0. Swiss rounds set by the
    Organizer (default ⌈log₂ N⌉), "Pair next round" when the round is
    done, pairing by score then rank avoiding rematches, editable before
    play. No colors. Entrants are Participants or Teams; either player
    self-reports; Placement Points from the final standings.
15. **Dynamic podium.** Show only the places a Competition decides.
    Without a 3rd place match the semifinal losers are not placed.
16. **Head-to-head** is exactly 2 Entrants, set by an Organizer or Host;
    Best of 1, 3, 5 or 7, required; logging is blocked once someone has
    the majority; a drawn series that runs out stays drawn, no extra
    matches.
17. **Best score** has no Entrant list and no enrollment: anyone in the
    War Week can log (when self-report is on); Organizers and Hosts log
    for anyone.
18. **No self-report on Placement** (Paul; ADR 0010 stands).
19. **Hosts are Participants** on this War Week's roster, not emails.
    No email needed to be chosen; access arrives once they have an email
    and sign in. XII data is test data: unmatched host rows are dropped.
20. **Award history** is grouped by Award name (case-insensitive).
21. **Bulk calls accepted.** All ranked tables including War Week
    standings use the one sortable table; one `ParticipantPicker`
    (avatar, name, Team; searches name and email) everywhere a
    Participant is chosen, absorbing backlog 108; the Organizers editor
    stays a typed email; check-in closing time cut too; Bracket match
    Scores become numeric (non-numeric existing values emptied and
    listed in the PR); Bracket Head-to-head / Group toggle; `/about`
    stills regenerated in XII theming; settings lock only on play started
    or Closed.
22. **Banned terms.** "Match" and "League" come off the banned list.
    "Match" was banned only while Heat and Game were the words.

## Glossary changes (CONTEXT.md)

- **Match** (new): one contest between Entrants inside a Bracket round,
  a Head-to-head series, or a League round. Replaces **Heat** and the
  Head-to-head **Game**.
- **Attempt** (new): one logged Score in a Best score Competition.
  Replaces the Best score **Game**.
- **Winner** replaces **Champion**.
- **Close / Reopen** for every Format; **Finalize**, **Un-finalize** go.
- **League** (new Format): round robin or Swiss.
- **Host**: a Participant on the War Week's roster assigned to a
  Competition.
- **Award preset** replaces **Award Category**.
- Removed: Logging closes, Enrollment closes, Check-in closes, Entrants
  open / fixed Entrant list (Games), Quick fill.
- Banned-terms table: drop **Match** and **League** (and the scan's lists).
